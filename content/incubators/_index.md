        Have you been wanting to apply Knowledge Graphs for use cases in your domain? Do you need support in such a process? The good news is that the KGI4NFDI project provides incubators, which are designed to facilitate Knowledge Graph implementation work via a structured process. Bring your use cases and some enthusiasm along the way and join us in this interesting process!

        ### How should you apply?

        1. Familiarize yourself with the incubator process, as shown below, or visit <https://zenodo.org/records/24274264>.

           ![KGI4NFDI incubator process: call announced, responses evaluated by the Expert Board, 10 NFDI projects selected, incubator cycle kick-off, six 4-week sprints over a 6-month cycle in collaboration with TS4NFDI, successful projects deliver KGs](incubator_process.png)

        2. Ensure you have the necessary resources to engage in the application scope you are proposing.
        3. Fill out the template [Template for KGI4NFDI Incubators](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bQJLLvriucLXyHT3KHNG2OU3TOPOhetGPQ76YgEO1PU/export?format=docx) (English or German), describe your incubator project idea and send it to us by mail (<kgi4nfdi@lists.nfdi.de>).
        4. You may be asked to briefly present your idea to the Incubator Board and answer additional questions.

        **Next steps:** We check all submissions and, if necessary, reserve them for following incubator cycles. The selection of incubator projects is made according to the criteria listed below, and on our available capacities.

        Some important dates to consider for this incubator cycle:

        | Activity                 | Date                 |
        |--------------------------|----------------------|
        | Call opens               | November 16          |
        | Application deadline     | December 31          |
        | Selection / notification | January 22           |
        | Incubator kick-off       | February 1           |
        | Incubator period         | February 1 – June 30 |
        | Final results            | July 15              |

        **Need more information?** For more information about the KGI4NFDI Base Service, its services, guidelines, and Knowledge Graph registry, please visit <https://kgi.services.base4nfdi.de/>, or join our regular consultancy sessions (every third Wednesday of the month from 15:00 to 16:00) to ask any further questions.

        Best regards,
        **The KGI4NFDI Team**

        Contact: <kgi4nfdi@lists.nfdi.de>
